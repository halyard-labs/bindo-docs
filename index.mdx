---
title: Bindo
description: An email inbox built around the customer, with an assistant you control.
---
Bindo helps small businesses manage enquiries without losing the customer's history. Incoming email is grouped by customer; individual enquiries track what still needs an answer.

The pilot includes email through Moonemail, a business knowledge base, a managed assistant, source-backed reply drafts and a human approval step before sending.
