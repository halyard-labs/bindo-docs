---
title: Set up your inbox
description: Start with email, a few useful answers and clear reply rules.
---
<Steps>
<Step title="Get workspace access">
Your Bindo administrator provisions workspace membership. Sign in with Google or email. If you belong to multiple workspaces, choose one in the sidebar. External agents use separate scoped workspace tokens.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect email">
Open **Channels**. Choose a dedicated receiving subdomain, add the DNS records shown, and verify both sending and receiving before activating the address. See [email setup](/guides/email).
</Step>
<Step title="Add business knowledge">
Open **Knowledge** and add your opening hours, common services, prices and policies. Publish entries you have checked. Set a review date on information that may expire.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe your business">
Open **Business**. Add business details and customer requests: the outcome to aim for, information to collect and when a person should take over. Under **Reply settings**, set the assistant's tone, remit, signature and handoff member. Your administrator configures the drafting provider; the current hosted pilot uses OpenAI through the Vercel AI SDK. Enable drafting when the business information is ready.
</Step>
<Step title="Review the first reply">
Open a customer, select an enquiry and draft from knowledge. Read the sources and any flags. Acknowledge flagged issues only after checking them, then approve the exact text to send.
</Step>
</Steps>

Connecting a domain does not import your old mailbox. Customer history grows from messages received by Bindo and replies sent through it.
