---
title: Billing
description: The monthly pilot plan, invoices and cancellation.
---
The Bindo pilot costs **A$99/month + GST (A$108.90 including Australian GST)** for one business workspace and **250 incoming enquiries per subscription month**. Onboarding is by invitation. There are **no automatic overage charges**; higher usage triggers a review with you before any plan change. Unused allowance does not roll over.

An enquiry is a new inbound email conversation. Follow-ups linked by email reply headers stay in that enquiry; a fresh, unlinked email can create another enquiry from the same customer. Duplicate deliveries do not count again. Identified spam and agreed onboarding test traffic are excluded during pilot usage review. The combined customer history is not the billing unit.

In **Settings → Billing**, review and accept the [terms](https://bindo.ai/terms), then continue to Stripe Checkout. Billing is enabled after your workspace is ready; existing pilot access is not charged without checkout. Stripe shows the final applicable tax before payment and hosts card entry. Bindo does not collect full card details.

Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. **Manage billing** opens Stripe's portal for invoices, payment methods and cancellation at the end of the paid period. Contact [support@bindo.ai](mailto:support@bindo.ai) before renewal if you cannot use the portal. Cancellation stops renewals; it does not automatically delete workspace data.

Subscription status comes from verified Stripe events, not the browser's return from Checkout. Open checkouts are reused and existing subscriptions block duplicate checkout. Disputes, refunds and fraud warnings require operator review. A payment failure does not automatically stop inbound support during the pilot.

See the [privacy policy](https://bindo.ai/privacy) for data handling and closure requests, and [Trust & security](https://bindo.ai/trust) for hosting and access controls.
